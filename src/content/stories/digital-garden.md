I wanted somewhere to put things that did not need to become announcements. A
photograph from a walk. A paragraph that had stayed in my notebook. A small
technical discovery that might help a stranger.

## A place with fewer counters

When I sketched this website, I left out the numbers. No follower count, no
public score for a photograph. The interesting question was whether someone
could find a thought worth staying with.

## Keeping the structure simple

Stories hold the longer threads. Moments hold the fragments: a few photographs
and just enough words to remember the day. Both belong to the same life, but
they ask for different kinds of attention.

> A personal website can be a place to return to, rather than another
> place to keep up with.

## Room to change

This corner of the internet will grow slowly. Some pages will remain
unfinished. I am learning to see that as a useful quality: a sign that the
person behind them is still paying attention.
